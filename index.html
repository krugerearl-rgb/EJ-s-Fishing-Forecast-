ℹ️ About EJ's Fishing Forecast

📡 Multi-Model Forecast Background Checks

This app runs real-time automated background checks against global atmospheric and marine forecast models (via Open-Meteo). It cross-references water temperatures, surface pressures, wind directions, swell heights, daily solar/lunar phases, and precipitation probability.

🌙 Night-Time Fishing Engine & Optimal Hours

Certain target predators feed heavily under dark cover. The engine analyzes nocturnal conditions—including pressure drops, wind comfort, rain probability, and tide alignment after sunset—up front to display dedicated 🌙 Night Peak feeding windows and bonus confidence ratings.

🎯 Dynamic Species Scoring Engine

Each target fish has ideal environmental thresholds. The app calculates a score out of 100 by matching current weather checks against preferred species habits.

🔍 Custom Species Fine-Tuning & Auto-Fill

When adding a custom target fish, you can specify tidal stages, light & night preferences, maximum swell limits, wind caps, and barometric pressure trends. Use "🔍 Auto-Fill Profile" to instantly fetch scientific baseline profiles!

🗺️ Google Maps Satellite Hybrid Pinpoint Picker

Powered by high-resolution Google Satellite tiles and embedded road & town name labels to pinpoint exact rock ledges, gullies, or estuary banks.

🧠 Smart Logbook Learning

Log your catches with photos and details. The scoring engine automatically gives bonus confidence points (+10) to target species you've successfully caught in past trips!
